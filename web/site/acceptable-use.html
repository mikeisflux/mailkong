Acceptable use

This is a plain-language summary of what you may and may not send. It is not a substitute for the full terms, and it is written to be read rather than skipped.

What Mailkong is for

Transactional mail: messages a person triggered or explicitly asked for. Receipts, password resets, order and shipping updates, account alerts, security notifications, one-to-one replies.

What you may not send

  • Mail to addresses you did not collect yourself, including purchased, scraped, rented or appended lists.
  • Bulk marketing or newsletters on the shared pool. Ask us about a dedicated IP instead.
  • Anything deceptive about who the sender is, including forged headers or look-alike domains.
  • Phishing, credential harvesting, malware, or links to any of the above.
  • Mail on behalf of a third party whose sending practices you cannot vouch for.

Limits we enforce automatically

These are not warnings — the system applies them without waiting for a human.

  • You cannot send from a domain until its SPF and DKIM records verify.
  • New accounts start with a low daily cap that rises automatically after a clean sending history.
  • Hard bounces and spam complaints are suppressed immediately and permanently until you remove them.
  • An account whose bounce or complaint rate spikes is paused, and a human reviews it.

A paused account keeps full dashboard access. You will be able to see exactly why, and the API will tell your application the reason rather than failing silently.

Reporting abuse

If you received mail from our infrastructure that you believe violates any of the above, write to abuse@mailkong.net. Reports are read by a person, and include enough for us to identify the sender in seconds.
