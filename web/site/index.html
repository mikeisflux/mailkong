Sending well is mostly setup. We did the setup.

SPF, DKIM, DMARC, reverse DNS, return paths, warmed IPs, bounce handling, complaint loops. It is a week of work to get right and a permanent job to keep right. You get it as four steps.

Add your domain

We generate your DKIM keypair and show you the exact DNS records to publish, with copy buttons and a live check that tells you the moment they resolve.

Grab a credential

An API key or an SMTP username and password. Point your app at smtp.mailkong.net:587 or the HTTP API — whichever your framework already speaks.

Send

Your mail leaves from IPs we own, with correct reverse DNS and a return path that actually collects bounces. Every message is searchable in your dashboard.

Listen

Signed webhooks for delivered, bounced, failed, opened and clicked. Hard bounces and complaints suppress themselves so you never hit the same dead address twice.

What you actually get

Not a feature list for its own sake — the things you will use in the first week.

✉

SMTP and HTTP API

Port 587 with STARTTLS for anything that already sends mail, or a JSON API for anything new. Same pipeline, same logs, same webhooks.

◎

Message search that works

Filter by recipient, status, tag, domain or date. Open any message for its full delivery timeline, raw headers and the exact bounce response from the receiving server.

⚡

Signed webhooks

HMAC-SHA256 over the payload with a timestamp, so you can verify it and reject replays. Retries with backoff, and a delivery log showing every attempt.

↩

Inbound routing

Point support@ or a catch-all subdomain at us and we POST parsed JSON to your endpoint. Replies become webhooks instead of a mailbox nobody reads.

⊘

Automatic suppression

Hard bounces and spam complaints are added to your suppression list the moment they happen, and blocked on the next send. Exportable, editable, per-account.

◈

Dedicated IPs when you need them

Start on our warmed shared pool. Move to an IP of your own — with its own reverse DNS and its own reputation — when your volume justifies it.

Questions people actually ask

Can I send marketing email?

Our shared pool is transactional only — receipts, resets, alerts, notifications. Mixing bulk campaigns into a transactional pool damages delivery for everyone on it. If you need to send campaigns, talk to us about a dedicated IP so your reputation is entirely your own.

What happens when I hit my monthly limit?

Sending stops and the API returns a clear 402 with the reason, rather than silently charging you. You will have had a warning in the dashboard and by email at 80%. Upgrade at any time and sending resumes immediately.

Do I have to move my domain's DNS to you?

No. Your domain stays wherever it is. You publish a handful of records — SPF, DKIM, and optionally DMARC and a return path — and we verify them. Nothing about your website or existing mail changes.

Can I keep receiving mail at the same domain?

Yes. Sending through us does not touch your MX records, so Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or whatever you use for your inbox keeps working exactly as it does now.

What if a message fails?

You get a webhook with the receiving server's exact response, and the message is in your activity log with its full delivery timeline. Hard bounces are added to your suppression list automatically so the next send skips that address.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Suppression lists export as CSV, message history is available through the API, and there is no lock-in beyond changing your SMTP credentials or API base URL back to whatever you used before.
