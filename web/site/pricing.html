Billing questions

What counts as a message?

One message to one recipient. A single API call addressed to three people counts as three. Inbound messages routed to your webhook do not count against your sending allowance.

What happens if I go over?

Sending stops rather than silently billing you. The API returns 402 with the reason, your dashboard shows it, and you will have been warned at 80% of your allowance. Upgrading takes effect immediately.

Can I change plans mid-month?

Yes, at any time. Stripe prorates the difference, and your new limits apply the moment the change goes through.

How much is a dedicated IP?

It is a Pro add-on, priced per IP per month, and it is not instant — we allocate the address, set its reverse DNS, and warm it gradually before your volume moves onto it. Request it from your dashboard and we will come back to you with timing.

Do you offer annual billing?

Not yet. Everything is month to month, cancel whenever, and your data is exportable on the way out.
